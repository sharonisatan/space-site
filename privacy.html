2. How Space works, and data we process for others

Space is self-hosted software. The Space app connects to a Space server run by you or your organisation (the "operator"), for example on the operator's own Mac or Linux machine. Messages, recordings, transcripts, notes, pages, files, voice profiles and call data ("Customer Data") are stored on that server, not on ours. The operator controls that Customer Data and decides who can access it.

Where the operator is you or your organisation, we do not receive Customer Data, and this Privacy Policy does not cover it. Please refer to your operator's privacy policy, or contact them, about that data.

Where we run a Space server on a customer's behalf, we act only as a data intermediary (processor) for that Customer Data, and process it only on behalf of and under the instructions of that customer. We do not train AI models on Customer Data unless the customer explicitly opts in.

4. The Space app and your device

The Space app processes the following on your device and sends it only to the Space server you sign in to:

  • Account details: your name, username, profile photo and sign-in token. The sign-in token is stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Messages and files: messages, threads, pages, comments, images and attachments you send.
  • Recordings and transcripts: audio you choose to record, and the transcripts, summaries and follow-ups made from it. Original recordings stay on your phone and are uploaded to your server.
  • Voice profiles: if you choose to save your voice, short samples and a voice profile used to recognise speakers in meeting notes. You can opt out, and you can use "Forget voice" to delete your samples at any time.
  • Calls: audio and video during calls, sent through the call server your operator runs. Calls are not recorded unless someone explicitly starts a recording, and all participants see a recording indicator.

The app asks for your permission before using your microphone (recording and calls), camera (calls and profile photos), photo library (sharing photos and choosing a profile photo) and local network (connecting to a Space server on your network). You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Transcription and summaries run on your Space server. Your content is not sent to us, or to a third-party AI service, unless your operator configures one. We do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

9. Security

We maintain reasonable physical, technical and administrative safeguards to protect personal data from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure or modification. Space requires sign-in for all access, checks conversation membership before showing messages, notes or files, and stores sign-in tokens in the iOS Keychain. Space does not currently offer end-to-end encryption, so the operator of a Space server can access content stored on it. Operators should use HTTPS when making a server available over the internet.

10. Retention of your personal data

We retain personal data only for as long as needed to fulfil the purposes in this policy, or as necessary for legal or business purposes. After that, it is anonymised (where permitted by law), deleted or destroyed. Customer Data on a Space server is kept until it is deleted by the user or operator.
